Hi Al,
I want to give you more specific direction because I do not want to bring in another designer. You’ve designed and developed too many strong experiences for us, and I know you’re capable of getting this where it needs to be.
The functionality and product architecture are strong. Please do not rebuild the product or remove the components you’ve created. This is primarily a UI/UX and visual-language redesign.
Overall Design Direction
I want this to feel like a real enterprise restaurant intranet/extranet and operating system that a GM, district manager, regional executive or corporate leader could realistically log into every day.
It should NOT look like:
an AI-generated website
a Lovable/Replit-style generated application
an AI startup landing page
a collection of oversized cards and gradients
Think: premium restaurant enterprise software + modern brand experience + operational command center.
The AI should power the experience, but the interface itself should not scream “AI.”
1. REMOVE THE MARKETING-STYLE LANDING PAGE
The current:
“Every location. Every shift. One command view.”
with the large gradient background and four large metric cards feels like an AI/SaaS landing page.
Instead, bring the user directly into a realistic restaurant operating environment.
For example:

 Ballantyne #02

or
BALLANTYNE #02 — OPERATIONS
Then immediately show what is happening in the restaurant.
We can still have a short Privé value statement somewhere, but it shouldn’t dominate the application.

gradients

3. USE RESTAURANT VISUAL LANGUAGE
This should immediately feel relevant to someone operating a restaurant.
Use restaurant/location imagery selectively—not stock photos everywhere, but enough visual context to make the environment feel alive.
Think about how Five Guys or another major restaurant brand feels visually.

But imagine that a Five Guys executive logged into the backend of their corporate site.
It should feel like their brand world continued into an enterprise operating environment.
That’s the feeling I’m after.
